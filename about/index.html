About me

I'm Harry Hallifax. When you work with Satellite Online, you're working with me.

A musician first

I'm a touring musician, so I know what it's like to be on the road, juggling gigs, rehearsals and travel, and still be expected to keep a website and a handful of social media accounts up to date. When I build something for a band, I build it to fit around that life, not the other way round. When im not building websites, you'll usually find me behind the drums with Vain Wild.

Social media from the inside

I have experience managing social media for myself and for bands, with my personal profiles standing at around 5,000 combined followers. I can help whether your goal is to let your mates know about your next show, promote an event or become the next big Tiktok influencer.

Technology that makes sense to the people using it

My specialism at University was how people interact with technology: building interfaces that make sense to the person using them, not just the person who built them. This makes me perfectly suited to making sure your website is easy to navigate for fans and easy to update for you.

You'll only ever deal with me

There are no account managers, no call centres and no being passed between departments. The person you first speak to is the person who does the work and the person you call if anything goes wrong.

Why that matters

Put together, it means you get someone who understands your world, explains things in plain English, and leaves you with something you can actually use once the job is done. You get to spend less time on the tech and more time on the music.
